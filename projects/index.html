Plug That In

Plug That In takes a different approach to battery notifications for macOS (MacTreasure).

During long hours of online work, I kept running out of battery while hyper-focused or in meetings where the battery indicator wasn’t visible. The core of the app is a notification that moves with your mouse cursor—impossible to miss, even when you’re deep in flow.
